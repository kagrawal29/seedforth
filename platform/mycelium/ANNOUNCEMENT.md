# Mycelium Launch — Team Access Now Live

Mycelium is live for the residency. The living graph is ready to learn with you.

You now have read access to the entire canonical system (prod) and the experimental frontier (dev), plus a writable local copy for prototyping. The system is self-maintaining — it detects its own gaps, heals unhealthy states, and learns from how it learns. You're joining an organism, not using a tool.

To start: `bash setup-team.sh` (5 minutes). Get your forest alias (Banyan, Sequoia, Birch, Cedar, Oak) and team passwords from 1Password. Then `mycelium --target prod ask "what is the system health status"` to hear the graph speak for itself. Full walkthrough: `docs/first-dogfood.md`.

The graph is the substrate. Your contributions are crystallized. Questions become knowledge. Patterns become protocols. Join in #mycelium-ops.
