# Explain Understanding Before Editing

Before editing any file or making changes, explain your understanding of the current state and your intended changes to the user. Wait for their confirmation before proceeding.

Users need to verify that you understood the request correctly. This is especially important for design changes, refactoring requests, and any task where the user's intent is subjective or could be interpreted multiple ways. A 30-second explanation prevents 5-minute reverts.

When the user says something like "change this to look better" or "fix the layout" — pause, explain what you think they mean, describe your plan, then ask "does this match what you had in mind?"
